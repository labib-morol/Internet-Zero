# Human blind playtest script

Print or read aloud only the parts marked SUBJECT. Keep everything else in front of the
observer. Fill results into the stuck-point table of the blind-playtesting skill.

## Setup (observer)

- Fresh guest session of the world (or a team of one if the world requires it).
- Session recorded or screen-shared; wall-clock timer running.
- You have: this script, a copy of the stuck-point table, the world's build report.
- You do NOT help. If asked for help, answer: "Play it the way you'd play any website."
  If they explicitly ask for a hint, give the next tier in order and note (gate, tier, minute).

## SUBJECT briefing (read verbatim)

"You are playing a short investigation game. A dead company's leftover servers just came
back online. Look around, collect what seems important, and file case reports when you
think you know things — the game tells you what it wants. Talk out loud about what you are
trying and why. There is no time limit; we will stop after about 40 minutes."

No other instructions. No mention of gates, scores, hints or hosts.

## During play (observer prompts, at most every 10 minutes)

1. "What are you trying to figure out right now?" (record answer verbatim)
2. At each report submission or gate attempt: note gate, time, what they based it on.
3. At each stall (>2 min without an action): note where, what they last viewed, what they
   say they are looking for.
4. Do not name gates, hosts or facts they haven't said themselves.

## Debrief (after the timer, SUBJECT answers first, then observer fills gaps)

1. What do you think the story was?
2. What was the hardest thing to find? What finally made it click?
3. Was there a moment you wanted to quit? Where?
4. Did any answer feel unfair or guessy?
5. Did you notice the hints? Did they help or spoil?
6. On a scale of 1–5, would you play the next case?

## Observer record to produce

- Minutes to first collect, first gate solved, each subsequent gate, ending.
- Hints requested: (gate, tier, minute, helped y/n).
- Wrong submissions per gate (count + the guesses verbatim — normalizer gold).
- Stuck-point rows with last artifacts viewed (from the activity feed or event log).
- Quotes: 3 verbatim lines, one confusion, one breakthrough, one feeling.
- Scorer-equivalent tally for human runs: gates solved, report groups accepted, hints used,
  minutes; do not invent tool-call numbers.
